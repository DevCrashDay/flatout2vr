FlatOut 2 in VR (SteamVR, e.g. Pico 4 + Virtual Desktop)
=========================================================

What it is: scripts\FO2HeadTrack.asi (settings in scripts\FO2HeadTrack.ini, log in scripts\FO2HeadTrack.log).
- Your headset's rotation and position move the in-game camera (6DoF), seated in the driver's seat.
- Real 3D from the game's depth buffer, so both eyes update at the full game frame rate.
- The picture is anchored where your head was when it was drawn, so turning your head feels like looking around.
- Round rev counter and speedometer plus a position / lap / lap time bar, fixed on the dashboard in 3D.
- The game's speed zoom (wider view at high speed) is switched off while driving; it feels wrong in VR.
- The mouse cursor is hidden while driving.

How to start
1. Put on the headset, connect Virtual Desktop, and start SteamVR.
2. Start FlatOut2.exe. (The mod never starts SteamVR itself; it connects once SteamVR runs.)
3. In a race the camera sits in the driver's seat automatically, whichever in-game camera is selected.
   Pick the bumper camera so the game does not draw your own car's body around you.
4. Sit straight, look ahead and press F8 to recenter.

Keys (while driving)
  F7                         head tracking on/off
  F8                         recenter
  F9                         save seat + screen adjustments
  F10                        SteamVR screen on/off
  Ctrl + Left/Right          seat left/right
  Ctrl + Up/Down             seat forward/back
  Ctrl + PageUp/PageDown     seat up/down
  Ctrl+Shift + Up/Down       screen bigger/smaller (fine-tunes the world scale)
  Ctrl+Shift + Left/Right    screen closer/farther
  Ctrl+Shift + PageUp/Down   screen higher/lower

First drive: finding the seat
- The seat position is measured from the middle of the car. FlatOut 2 cars differ a lot in size, so
  adjust it with Ctrl + arrows / PageUp / PageDown until you sit where the driver sits, then press F9.

HUD
- Dashboard: rev counter (with gear) and speedometer, with the position / lap / lap time bar above them.
  Position, size, max rpm, redline and max speed: [GAUGES] in the ini.
  Their look comes from PNG files in the gauge_mod folder (see gauge_mod\README.txt to make your own).
- With [GAUGES] Enabled=0 a flat speed + gear panel and the race bar are drawn on the screen instead.

Stereo modes ([STEREO] Mode in the ini)
- Mode=1 (default): every game frame is drawn once and split into a left and right view using the game's
  depth buffer, so both eyes update at the full game rate. Each eye gets half the picture width, and edges
  of near objects can smear a little.
- Mode=0: the eyes take turns (left on one frame, right on the next): exact geometry and full width,
  but each eye only gets half the game's frame rate.

DXVK
- Put DXVK's 32-bit d3d9.dll (from the x32 folder of a DXVK release) and dxvk.conf in the game folder.
  Direct3D 9 then runs on Vulkan, and the finished frames go straight to SteamVR on the graphics card
  (no desktop capture, works in the background, lower latency).
- Without DXVK the mod falls back to capturing the game window from the desktop.
- Undo DXVK: delete d3d9.dll and dxvk.conf.

Smoothness
- The game runs as a real SteamVR app: each eye's picture is handed to SteamVR together with the head
  pose it was drawn with, and SteamVR keeps it steady between game frames (VRFrames=1 in the ini).
- Turn off the game's motion blur and its own force feedback (if you use FO2Telemetry for the wheel).
- More fps helps: an fps unlocker for FlatOut 2 works together with this mod.

Tuning
- If the world seems to slide when you turn your head, use Ctrl+Shift+Up/Down until it stays still, then F9.
- Wider or narrower view: GameFOV in [STEREO]; the screen grows with it.
- 3D too strong or too weak: DepthScale in [STEREO].
- Without a headset the mod only applies the seat position (you can still drive from the cockpit on the monitor).

Undo: delete scripts\FO2HeadTrack.asi and scripts\FO2HeadTrack.ini.
